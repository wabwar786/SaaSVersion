How scan-to-order works

1
Guest scans the QR on the table

Opens your branded web menu instantly in the phone browser — nothing to install.

2
They browse & add to cart

Live menu, prices and photos, pulled from the same Menu screen your staff manage.

3
Order drops into the kitchen

The KOT prints and the order appears on the KDS and Running Orders screen automatically.

4
Pay at table or on phone

Cash, card or Raast — with an FBR digital receipt sent over WhatsApp.

Generate a unique QR for every table and takeaway counter from this screen.
