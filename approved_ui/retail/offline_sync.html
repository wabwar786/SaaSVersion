Offline version

Counter PC ke liye offline software

Yeh package aapke store ke PC par chalta hai. Bill, stock, khata aur receipt — sab kuch internet ke baghair chalta hai. Jab internet aata hai, sara data khud-ba-khud cloud par chala jata hai. Package mein aapke business ka naam, branch aur sync token pehle se seal hain — kuch configure nahi karna.

Install kaise karein

Counter PC par, ek dafa

1
ZIP download kar ke extract karein

Counter PC par kisi bhi folder mein — misal C:\RetailOS. Network drive par na rakhein.

2
START_RESTAURANT.bat par double click

Pehli dafa PHP, MariaDB aur database khud install/taiyar hote hain. Kuch minute lagte hain — CMD ka koi ilm nahi chahiye.

3
Browser khud khul jayega

Wahi email aur password jo aapko yahan online diya gaya tha.

4
Hardware laga dein

Barcode scanner (keyboard-wedge — koi driver nahi), receipt printer, cash drawer aur label printer. Setup Printers & Devices se.

5
Sync khud chalti rahegi

Internet aate hi bills upar jate hain aur head-office ka catalog neeche aata hai. Kuch dabana nahi parta.

Kaunse modules sirf offline chalte hain

Kuch cheezein counter ke hardware se judi hain — cloud se un tak pohancha hi nahi ja sakta

ModuleKahan chalta haiWajah
Tax / Digital Invoice (FBR, PRA, KPRA) Sirf offline FBR ka fiscal service usi PC par hota hai jahan POS chal raha hai (localhost). Cloud us tak pohanch hi nahi sakta.

Barcode & shelf label printing Sirf offline TSPL/ZPL label printer local device hai.
Weighing scale (live read) Sirf offline Scale serial/USB se juda hota hai. Scale ka chhapa hua label cloud par bhi scan ho jata hai — us mein weight barcode ke andar hota hai.
